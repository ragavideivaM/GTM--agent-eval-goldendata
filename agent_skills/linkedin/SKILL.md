# LinkedIn post

- Write 100–350 words with a useful opening and readable short paragraphs.
- Explain why the selected developments matter to busy professionals.
- Use a conversational, credible tone and avoid unexplained jargon.
- End with the campaign-appropriate CTA.
- Keep factual evidence references in structured metadata, not reader-facing copy.
